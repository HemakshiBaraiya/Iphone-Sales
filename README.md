### Project Overview
The primary objective of this project is to analyze and compare the global launch performance of the iPhone 14 and iPhone 15 series across 10 international 
markets over their initial 3-month launch periods (September to November).

### Data Source 
Data Contain three datasets for this analysis:
1. dim_stores
2. fact_sales_iPhone14
3. fact_sales_iPhone15

### Tools Used 
**Power BI Desktop:** Data transformation, DAX modeling, and multi-page interactive dashboard development.

### Key Insights

**Overall Volume:** Total launch-window sales dipped slightly from 1.23M units (iPhone 14) to 1.18M units (iPhone 15), representing a -4.26% YoY variance.   

**Regional Shift:** 
- The United States was the dominant market for iPhone 14 (269K units / 21.8% share), but experienced a -33% YoY contraction in the iPhone 15 cycle.   
- Germany emerged as the top-performing market for iPhone 15, surging +15.5% YoY to 224K units.   
- High-momentum growth was also recorded in Brazil (+19.7%) and India (+11.3%).   

**Sales Momentum (MoM):** iPhone 14 experienced its peak surge in October (+6.63% MoM), whereas iPhone 15 maintained steadier late-quarter resilience through November (+0.46% MoM).
